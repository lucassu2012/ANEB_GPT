# ANEB domain context

## Quick evidence collection

A bounded Token, Realtime, or Network execution that freezes one exact App,
server, Profile, run identity, raw evidence, cleanup receipt, verification
report, and final READY sibling. A Quick evidence collection proves only its
declared narrow execution contract; it is not a formal experience baseline.

## Mechanical lifecycle

The family-neutral order `preflight -> acquire -> collect -> phone cleanup ->
remote cleanup -> publish`. It owns ordering, failure retention, cleanup
requirements, and publish eligibility. It does not own family business
semantics.
## Family adapter

The Token, Realtime, or Network implementation at the mechanical-lifecycle
seam. A family adapter owns its immutable identity, live handles, business
markers, evidence derivation, and business verifier. Family adapters must not
inherit another family's identity or business defaults.

## Publish eligibility

The mechanical decision that publication may be attempted only after the
required preflight, acquisition, collection, phone cleanup, and remote cleanup
outcomes are all valid. Publish eligibility is not publication success; READY
and its independent release consumer remain the final publication proof.

## Business semantics

Family-specific meaning such as Token 20/3/1 behavior, Realtime duplex-frame
cadence, Network HTTP/UDP counts, receipt-missing rejection, KPI, AQS, and
conclusions. Business semantics stay behind each family adapter and are never
generalized by the mechanical lifecycle.
